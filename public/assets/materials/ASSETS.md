# Generated architectural materials

These local surface maps were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this branch's spatial material system. They are not résumé or project poster assets.

| Files | Prompt summary | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lime-plaster.webp`, `lime-plaster-height.webp` | Seamless hand-troweled warm lime plaster, flat orthographic surface, even neutral lighting, sand/cream mineral grain, no objects, text, or shadows. | Villa facade and interior envelopes |
| `walnut-herringbone.webp`, `walnut-herringbone-height.webp` | Seamless dark walnut herringbone parquet, flat orthographic surface, deep walnut/umber/honey grain, no room, objects, gloss, or perspective. | Floors, doors, window frames, and trim |
| `terracotta-roof.webp`, `terracotta-roof-height.webp` | Seamless Mediterranean barrel roof tiles, flat top-down surface, burnt sienna/clay/umber seams, no house, sky, or perspective. | Villa roof and ridge |
| `showroom-rug.webp`, `showroom-rug-height.webp` | Complete top-down modernist handwoven wool rug based on pathways, thresholds, and arches; rust, moss, teal, walnut, parchment, and lavender; no text, furniture, or mockup. | Public showroom and private bedroom rugs |

The `*-height.webp` variants are monochrome derivatives used as subtle bump maps. All final images are stored as optimized WebP files and load locally at runtime.
